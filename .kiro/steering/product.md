# Product: Slow Down

Slow Down is an Android app (`com.slowdown.app`). When you doom scroll too long, it slows your internet on purpose, so the feed stops being fun. When you leave, speed comes back.

## Idea
The idea came from the author's commute. A slow connection made them stop scrolling. Dial-up and 2G speeds are "dead" tech. Here they are revived as a digital wellbeing tool (Kiroween 2026, Resurrection category).

## How it works for the user
1. Pick watched apps, a time threshold (5 to 120 min), a speed (32 to 1024 kbps; presets Dial-up, 2G, 3G, Slow 4G) and a release time (1 to 30 min).
2. The app watches foreground time. At the threshold it slows the network for those apps and posts a notification with Stop.
3. After the user is away for the release time, speed returns.
4. Manual "Slow me down now" with a 15/30/60/none timer.

## Hero states
Off, Watching, Slowed.

## Tone
Calm and a little dry. No guilt, no shaming. Short sentences. Example: "Time to look up."

## Principles
- Nothing leaves the device. No account, no analytics, no server.
- The user can always stop it in one tap (Home button or notification).
- Say what is measured and what is not. Do not oversell.
